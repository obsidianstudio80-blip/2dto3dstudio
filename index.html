Pakistan construction uses a hybrid of traditional local units and the metric (SI) system. You must be fluent in both — drawings may use metric while clients and labourers speak in local units.

Rate Analysis Framework

Rate analysis = breaking down the cost of 1 unit of any work item into material + labour + equipment + overhead. This is how you build your own unit rates.

ComponentHow to CalculateTypical %Example (1 m³ RCC M15)
MaterialsQty of each material × market price50–65%Cement + Sand + Aggregate + water
LabourNo. of workers × daily wage × days per unit20–35%Mason, helper, carpenter, bar bender
EquipmentHire rate × hours per unit5–15%Mixer, vibrator, pump, crane
Small tools2–3% of material cost1–3%Spades, buckets, wheelbarrows
Contractor overhead8–12% of (M+L+E)8–12%Office, supervision, insurance
Profit margin8–15% of total cost8–15%Negotiated; competitive market

Golden Rule of Estimation

Measure twice. Price once. Always verify your quantities against a simple sanity check — cost per m² of BUA. If a number looks wrong, it probably is. Go back and check before submitting. A good estimate is not the fastest one — it's the most accurate one.
